Auto Fare Fixer (AFF)

Know your fare. Check whether an auto driver's demand is fair under the Telangana official tariff, and draft a complaint letter if it is not.

Open the web app Download the Android app (APK) View on GitHub

Use it in your browser, or install the Android app (allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser first). Web trips are saved in that browser only.

What it does

  • Estimates the fare for your distance using the official tariff, with a clear breakdown
  • Gives a verdict on the demanded fare: Fair, Slightly high or Overcharged
  • Drafts a short complaint letter with AI and lets you share it
  • Saves your trips, shows monthly analytics, and exports them as CSV
  • Includes SOS and a trusted contact

Telangana auto tariff used

First 1.6 km₹30
Each extra km₹14
Night (11 pm to 5 am)1.5x
Waiting₹0.75 per minute
Based on press reports of the 2 Oct 2026 order. Luggage charges are not modelled and tariffs can change.

Good to know

The verdict uses the official tariff. The machine-learning fare estimate is a prototype trained on practice data and is shown only as a reference until it is retrained on real trips. Complaint letters are drafts and are not sent to any authority.

Auto Fare Fixer is an independent student project and is not affiliated with the Telangana government.
